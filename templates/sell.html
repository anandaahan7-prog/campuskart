How the AI helps

Price model
A Linear Regression model trained on all listings in the database learns what percentage of the MRP items keep, based on condition, age and category.

Spam check
A Naive Bayes text classifier, plus rules for phone numbers, links and unrealistic prices, checks your listing. Suspicious listings go to the admin for approval.

Recommendations
Your item will be recommended to students who searched for similar things.
